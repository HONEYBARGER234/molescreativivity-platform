FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions

Here are some answers to questions users may have about the platform.

What is Molescreativivity? +

Molescreativivity is a digital platform designed around creative services, digital products, websites and other online solutions.

Can I purchase digital services? +

Yes. The marketplace and services sections are designed to allow users to discover and purchase available digital services.

How does the wallet work? +

The wallet interface is designed to display balances, deposits, transactions and purchases. Actual wallet functionality will be connected when the platform backend and payment system are integrated.

How does the referral system work? +

Registered users will eventually receive unique referral links and be able to track eligible referrals and rewards.

Can I sell my own digital products? +

The platform can be expanded to support creators and sellers listing digital products after the marketplace backend is connected.

How do I contact Molescreativivity? +

Use the contact section below to reach the Molescreativivity support team.
